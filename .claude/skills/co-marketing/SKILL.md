# Co-Marketing

Launch joint campaigns with partners who share your audience.

**Best partners share your audience but don't compete for the same budget.**

## Partner Identification

Look for companies that are **adjacent in your customer's workflow** — either before, after, or alongside your product.

Sources for partner discovery:
- Your customers' tool stacks (survey or interview them)
- Integrations you've already built
- Complementary products in your category
- Tools with overlap audience on Product Hunt, G2

## Partner Scoring Framework

Score each potential partner 1–5:

| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|---------|
| Audience fit | Do they serve the same ICP? |
| Audience size | Can they drive meaningful reach? |
| Brand alignment | Do values and quality match? |
| Engagement quality | Active, engaged audience or dead list? |
| Reciprocity potential | Can we provide equal or greater value? |
| Execution ease | Will they actually follow through? |

Prioritize partners scoring 22+ out of 30.

## Campaign Types by Effort

**Low effort (start here):**
- Newsletter swap: you promote them, they promote you
- Guest post swap: 1,500-word article on each other's blog
- Social shoutout: coordinated LinkedIn/Twitter posts

**Medium effort:**
- Joint webinar: co-host on a shared topic
- Co-created content: joint research report or guide
- Email sequence: each promotes the other's lead magnet

**High effort (high reward):**
- Integrated product launch: build native integration + announce together
- Co-hosted conference or event
- Joint case study featuring a mutual customer

## Partnership Structure

Before launching any campaign, align on:

| Item | Define Upfront |
|------|---------------|
| Lead ownership | Who owns the lead list? Both parties? |
| Promotion commitments | Exact channels, dates, estimated reach |
| Asset creation | Who builds what, who reviews |
| Timeline | Draft deadlines, review windows, launch date |
| Success metrics | What does a good outcome look like? |
| Revenue share | If applicable: how are deals attributed? |

Write it down — even a simple email confirmation. Verbal agreements cause misalignment.

## Outreach Template

```
Subject: Partnership idea — [Your Company] × [Their Company]

Hi [Name],

We serve a lot of the same customers — [their ICP]. 
I've been impressed with [specific thing about their product/content].

I think there's a natural [newsletter swap / joint webinar / guide] 
opportunity where we could each reach [X] new potential customers.

[2-sentence value prop: what they'd get]

Worth 20 minutes to explore?

[Name]
```

## Measurement

Track per campaign:
- Reach (emails sent, social impressions)
- Engagement (click rate, webinar attendance)
- Leads generated (form fills, signups)
- Revenue attributed (30/60/90 day window)
- Relationship quality (would you work together again?)
