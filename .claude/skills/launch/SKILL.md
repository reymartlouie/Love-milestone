# Launch Strategy

Plan and execute product launches across five phases.

**Key insight:** "The best companies don't just launch once — they launch again and again." Launch isn't a single moment; it's a phased process building momentum.

## ORB Channel Framework

**Owned** — email list, blog, community, in-app notifications
→ Build these. They compound. High control, low reach initially.

**Rented** — social media, app stores, marketplaces
→ Drive traffic here back to owned channels. High reach, low control.

**Borrowed** — guest posts, influencers, press, partnerships
→ Tap existing audiences proactively. High reach, zero ownership.

Always convert borrowed/rented attention into owned relationships.

## Five-Phase Launch Process

| Phase | Goal | Audience |
|-------|------|----------|
| 1. Internal | Test with early users one-on-one | Internal team |
| 2. Alpha | External validation + landing page signup | 10–50 trusted users |
| 3. Beta | Scale testing + buzz generation | 50–500 users |
| 4. Early Access | Validate at scale | 500–5,000 waitlist |
| 5. Full Launch | Open signups, charge, announce broadly | Public |

## Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Landing page live with clear value prop
- [ ] Email list with welcome sequence
- [ ] Owned channels set up and active
- [ ] Launch assets created (screenshots, demo video, copy)
- [ ] Press kit ready
- [ ] Social proof collected (testimonials, case studies)
- [ ] Product Hunt hunter identified (if using)
- [ ] Partner announcements coordinated

## Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Announce across all channels (staggered, not simultaneous)
- [ ] Real-time engagement — reply to every comment
- [ ] Monitor for bugs or onboarding issues
- [ ] Email list announcement
- [ ] Post in relevant communities (Reddit, Slack groups, etc.)

## Post-Launch

- [ ] Onboarding sequence activated
- [ ] Comparison/alternative pages live
- [ ] Gather feedback and testimonials
- [ ] Plan next launch moment (new feature, milestone, press)

## Product Hunt Strategy

Success requires:
1. Pre-launch relationship-building with PH community (weeks in advance)
2. Polished listing (quality screenshots, demo GIF, clear tagline)
3. All-day engagement — reply to every comment
4. Convert PH traffic into email signups — upvotes alone don't matter
5. Coordinate supporter list before launch day
