# Copy Editing

Improve existing copy through seven focused sweeps. Don't rewrite — enhance.

**Before editing:** Check `.agents/product-marketing.md` for voice/tone guidelines.

**Five questions to ask first:**
1. What's the primary goal of this piece?
2. Who is the target reader?
3. What's the one action we want them to take?
4. What's the brand voice?
5. Are there any constraints (character limits, required claims)?

---

## The Seven Sweeps

Run each sweep as a separate pass. Don't try to fix everything at once.

### Sweep 1 — Clarity
Can a stranger understand this in one read?
- Replace jargon with plain language
- Break up complex sentences (max ~20 words)
- Ensure each paragraph has one idea

### Sweep 2 — Voice & Tone
Does this sound like the brand?
- Check against brand guidelines
- Consistent formality level throughout
- Remove out-of-character phrases

### Sweep 3 — "So What?"
Has every feature been connected to a benefit?
- "We have X" → "Which means you get Y"
- "Feature X" → "So you can Z"
- If a sentence doesn't advance the reader toward the goal, cut it

### Sweep 4 — "Prove It"
Is every claim supported?
- Vague: "industry-leading" → Specific: "rated #1 by [source]"
- "saves time" → "saves 3 hours/week (based on customer survey)"
- Unsupported superlatives: delete or add evidence

### Sweep 5 — Specificity
Replace vague language with concrete details:
- "Many customers" → "500+ teams"
- "Faster" → "2x faster"
- "Easy setup" → "Live in 5 minutes"
- Numbers and names are always more convincing than adjectives

### Sweep 6 — Emotional Resonance
Does this connect to what readers actually care about?
- Name their pain explicitly
- Paint the better future state
- Match emotional register (urgent problem = urgent tone)

### Sweep 7 — Zero Friction
Have we removed all barriers to action?
- Is the CTA clear and single?
- Have we addressed the most obvious objection?
- Is there a risk reversal (trial, guarantee, free tier)?

---

## After Editing

Verify previous sweeps weren't undone by later edits. Read aloud — if it sounds awkward, it reads awkward.

## Quick Pass (lower stakes content)

For social posts, short ads, email subject lines — run sweeps 1, 3, and 5 only.
