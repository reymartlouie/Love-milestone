# Competitor Pages

Create comparison and alternative pages that rank for competitive queries and convert.

**Goal:** Rank for "[Competitor] alternative" and "[Us] vs [Them]" searches while building trust through honesty.

## Four Page Formats

| Format | Slug | When to Use |
|--------|------|------------|
| [Competitor] Alternative | `/[competitor]-alternative` | Targeting one competitor's users |
| [Competitor] Alternatives | `/[competitor]-alternatives` | Targeting early-stage researchers |
| [Us] vs [Competitor] | `/[us]-vs-[competitor]` | Direct positioning, head-to-head |
| [A] vs [B] | `/[a]-vs-[b]` | Neutral comparison, you're option C |

## Honest Comparison Principles

- **Acknowledge competitor strengths** — readers will fact-check, dishonesty destroys trust
- **Be specific about your limitations** — "We don't have X yet" is credible
- **Use verifiable claims** — no "best in class" without proof
- **Include real pricing** — dated, with disclaimers

Readers are evaluating. They know both products. Spin doesn't convert — clarity does.

## Alternative Page Structure

```
H1: [Competitor] Alternative — [Your Product]
Subhead: [Who this is for and why they switch]

## Why people look for [Competitor] alternatives
[1-3 honest reasons: cost, missing feature, complexity]

## [Your Product] vs [Competitor] — Key Differences
[Comparison table: features, pricing, ideal use case]

## What [Your Product] does better
[3 specific advantages with evidence]

## What [Competitor] does better
[1-2 honest areas where competitor wins]
[Use this to self-qualify: "If X matters most, [Competitor] may be a better fit"]

## Pricing comparison
[Current pricing for both, dated]

## Migration
[How easy is it to switch? What's the process?]

## Customer stories
[Customers who switched, specific outcomes]

## Start your trial
[CTA]
```

## SEO Optimization

- Primary keyword: `[competitor] alternative` or `[competitor] vs [your product]`
- Include competitor name in title tag, H1, first paragraph
- Internal links to: pricing page, features page, relevant case studies
- Update quarterly — pricing and features change

## Sales Enablement Use

These pages also work as sales collateral when prospects are evaluating both products. Share the honest comparison in sales calls — it builds credibility.
