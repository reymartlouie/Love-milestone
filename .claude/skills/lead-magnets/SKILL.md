# Lead Magnets

Plan and create lead magnets that capture emails and qualify leads.

## Format Selection by Effort & Stage

| Format | Effort | Best For |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Checklist | 1–2 hours | Awareness stage, high conversion |
| Template | 2–4 hours | Consideration stage, qualified leads |
| Cheat sheet | 2–4 hours | Awareness, quick wins |
| Email course | 1–2 days | Nurture over time |
| Webinar | 1–3 days | High-intent leads, demo opportunity |
| Guide / Ebook | 1–3 weeks | Authority building, SEO |
| Calculator / Tool | Varies | High intent, specific problem |
| Assessment | 1–2 days | Personalization, segmentation |

## Buyer Stage Matching

| Stage | Content Type | Goal |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Awareness | Guides, checklists, cheat sheets | Educate, capture interest |
| Consideration | Comparisons, templates, assessments | Help evaluate, qualify intent |
| Decision | Trials, demos, ROI calculators | Remove last objection |

## Gating Strategy

**Minimize friction:**
- Ask for email only on first capture
- Every extra field reduces conversion by 5–10%
- Progressive profiling: collect more data in subsequent interactions

**What to gate vs. not:**
- Gate: high-value, specific, actionable content
- Don't gate: content that builds awareness (blog posts, basic guides)

## Lead Magnet Landing Page

Essential elements:
1. **Headline** — specific outcome: "The 12-point launch checklist used by 500+ SaaS founders"
2. **What they get** — bullet list of specific deliverables
3. **Preview** — screenshot or first page visible
4. **Social proof** — "Downloaded 10,000+ times" or testimonial
5. **Single form field** — email only
6. **CTA** — "Send me the checklist" (not "Submit")

Target conversion: 20–40% for warm traffic (your own audience).

## Distribution Channels

- **Content upgrades** — offer related lead magnet within blog posts
- **Exit-intent popup** — catch visitors before they leave
- **Social media** — promote the outcome, not the lead magnet itself
- **Paid ads** — great for cold audience if lead magnet is highly specific
- **Partner promotions** — co-market with complementary audiences
- **Email signature** — passive, ongoing

## Measuring Success

| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|
| Landing page conversion | 20–40% (warm traffic) |
| Cost per lead (paid) | <$5 B2C, <$50 B2B |
| Lead-to-trial | >10% for well-targeted |
| Lead-to-customer | Varies by price point |
