# Marketing Ideas

139 proven SaaS marketing ideas organized by category, stage, budget, and timeline.

## Process

1. Learn about product, audience, and current stage
2. Suggest 3–5 tailored strategies with rationale
3. Walk through implementation steps
4. Match ideas to available resources and timeline

## Quick Navigation

| Goal | Best Ideas |
|------|-----------|
| Leads fast | Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Free Tools, Cold Email |
| Building authority | Speaking, Podcasts, Thought Leadership, Research Reports |
| Low/no budget | SEO, Reddit, Comment Marketing, Community |
| Product-led growth | Viral loops, In-app upsells, Powered-by marketing |
| Long-term compounding | SEO, Email list, Content Strategy, Referrals |

## Idea Categories

- **Content & SEO** — blog, hub pages, programmatic SEO, link building
- **Free Tools** — calculators, generators, analyzers as lead gen
- **Paid Ads** — Google, Meta, LinkedIn, retargeting
- **Social & Community** — Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Discord
- **Email** — newsletters, sequences, cold outreach
- **Partnerships** — co-marketing, integrations, affiliates
- **Events** — webinars, conferences, AMAs
- **PR** — HARO, journalist relationships, product launches
- **Product-Led Growth** — freemium, viral features, sharing mechanics
- **Sales** — cold email, prospecting, SDR sequences

## Context Needed

To suggest the right ideas:
- What's the product and ICP?
- Current stage (pre-launch, early, growth, scale)?
- Monthly marketing budget?
- Team size and available time?
- What's already been tried?

## Output

3–5 prioritized ideas with:
- Why it fits your situation
- Step-by-step implementation
- Expected timeline to results
- Estimated effort and cost
