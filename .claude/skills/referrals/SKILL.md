# Referral & Affiliate Programs

Turn customers into a growth engine.

**Key stat:** Referred customers have **16–25% higher LTV** and **18–37% lower churn** than other acquisition channels.

## The Referral Loop

```
Trigger Moment → Share Action → Convert Referred → Reward → (Repeat)
```

## Two Program Types

| Type | Who refers | Best for |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Customer referral | Existing customers | B2C, PLG, SMB SaaS |
| Affiliate program | Content creators, partners | B2B, high-ACV, media |

## Step 1: Identify Trigger Moments

Best moments to ask for a referral:
- **Post-aha moment** — right after they first get value
- **After milestones** — "You just sent your 100th email!"
- **Post-upgrade** — they just committed more money
- **After positive NPS** — they said they'd recommend you

Never ask at signup or during frustration.

## Step 2: Design Share Mechanism

| Mechanism | Best For |
|-----------|---------|
| In-product share button | Highest conversion (lowest friction) |
| Unique referral link | Digital products, easy to track |
| Referral code | Works offline, easy to remember |
| Email template | B2B, higher-touch |

## Step 3: Choose Incentive Structure

| Structure | How It Works | Best For |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Single-sided | Referrer gets reward | Highly loyal customers |
| Double-sided | Both referrer + referee get reward | Consumer, viral loops |
| Tiered | More referrals = better rewards | Power users, advocates |
| Cash | % commission or flat fee | Affiliates, B2B |
| Credits | Account credit toward product | SaaS, subscription |
| Gift card | Third-party reward | When product credits don't motivate |

**B2B affiliate benchmark:** 20–30% revenue share for first 12 months

## Step 4: Build the Mechanics

Minimum viable referral program:
1. Unique tracking link per referrer
2. Attribution: cookie + email-based fallback
3. Automated reward delivery (don't make people chase)
4. Dashboard: referrer sees their stats and rewards
5. Terms: when rewards are paid, what counts as a conversion

## Measuring Program Health

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Referral rate | 5–15% of customers refer someone |
| Referred conversion | Match or beat organic |
| Viral coefficient | >1.0 = organic growth |
| CAC via referral | 50–80% lower than paid |
