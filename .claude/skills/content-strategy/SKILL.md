# Content Strategy

Plan content that drives traffic, builds authority, and generates leads.

## Before Planning

Check `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Also gather:
- Current content inventory (what exists, what's working)
- Target audience and their questions
- Competitive content landscape
- Available resources (writers, frequency, budget)

## Core Framework

Every piece targets one of two demand types:

**Searchable demand** — optimized for active searches (people looking for answers)
- Use-case content, how-to guides, comparison pages, templates
- Foundation of content strategy — builds compounding traffic

**Shareable demand** — spreads ideas (people share because it's interesting)
- Thought leadership, original data, expert roundups, case studies
- Amplifies reach but doesn't compound like SEO

Prioritize searchable first. Add shareable when you have capacity.

## Content Pillars

Define 3–5 core topics that:
- Your audience actively searches for
- You have genuine expertise in
- Connect to your product's value

Each pillar becomes a hub with supporting spoke content.

## Prioritization Scoring

Score each content idea:

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Customer impact (solves real problem) | 40% |
| Content-market fit (can you do it well?) | 30% |
| Search potential (keyword volume + intent) | 20% |
| Resource requirements (effort to produce) | 10% |

## Research Sources

- Keyword tools (search volume, difficulty, intent)
- Customer call transcripts and support tickets
- Reddit, Quora, G2 reviews — unfiltered customer language
- Competitor content gaps
- Sales team's most common questions

## Output

1. **Content pillars** (3–5 topics with rationale)
2. **Priority topics** (keyword, buyer stage, research backing, estimated impact)
3. **Topic cluster map** (hub page + spoke articles per pillar)
4. **Content calendar** (first 30/60/90 days)
