# Community Marketing

Build communities where members stay for the people, not just the product.

**Core philosophy:** "Value must flow to members first." Every touchpoint should benefit participants. Build around shared identity, not your product.

## Platform Selection

| Platform | Best For |
|----------|---------|
| Discord | Real-time, developer/creator communities, younger audience |
| Slack | B2B professional communities, less casual |
| Circle | Course-based, paid communities, structured content |
| Reddit | High-volume, public, SEO value |
| Forum (Discourse) | Long-form technical, searchable, evergreen |

## Launch Strategy

**Do unscalable work first:**
1. Recruit 20–50 founding members personally (DM, email, 1:1)
2. Pre-seed conversations before public launch (post content, answer questions yourself)
3. Set explicit culture norms (pinned post, onboarding message)
4. Reply to every post in first 30 days

Don't open to public until founding members are active and culture is set.

## Community Flywheel

```
Members join → gain value → engage → attract others → (repeat)
```

The 90% rule: 1% of members typically drive most activity. Design for the lurkers (they still get value and eventually become buyers), but invest in the 1%.

## Content Programming

Weekly rhythm example:
- **Monday:** Weekly question or challenge post
- **Wednesday:** Resource share (tool, article, tip)
- **Friday:** Win / showcase post ("Show us what you built")
- **Ongoing:** Reply to every member post within 24 hours

## Onboarding New Members

Within 24 hours of joining:
- Welcome message (from real person, not bot)
- Point them to 3 most valuable existing threads
- Ask one question to get them engaging

## Health Metrics

| Metric | Healthy Target |
|--------|---------------|
| DAU/MAU ratio | >20% |
| New member post within 7 days | >30% |
| Thread reply rate | >50% get at least 1 reply |
| Staff-driven content | <30% (members should drive most) |

## Red Flags

- Company team dominates posts (community feels like a newsletter)
- Questions unanswered for 24+ hours
- Same 5 people drive 80%+ of engagement
- Members joining but not posting (bad onboarding)

## Transition: Company-Led → Member-Led

Phase 1 (0–3 months): Company drives everything
Phase 2 (3–6 months): Identify 3–5 power members, give them tools + recognition
Phase 3 (6+ months): Power members lead programs, company facilitates
