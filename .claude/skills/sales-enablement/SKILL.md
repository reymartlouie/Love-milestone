# Sales Enablement

Create sales collateral that reps actually use.

**Key principle:** "Sales uses what sales trusts." If reps rewrite your deck before sending it, you wrote the wrong deck. Involve reps in creation. Use their language.

## Before Starting

Check `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Then gather:
- Specific collateral type needed
- End user (AE, SDR, SE, or champion)
- Sales stage (prospecting, discovery, demo, evaluation, close)
- Target persona and their top 3 objections
- Feedback on existing materials (what's missing or wrong)

## Asset Types by Sales Stage

| Stage | Asset |
|-------|-------|
| Prospecting | Cold email templates, LinkedIn sequences, one-pager |
| Discovery | Discovery question guide, persona cards |
| Demo | Demo script, use case library |
| Evaluation | Battle cards, ROI calculator, technical docs |
| Proposal | Proposal template, pricing guide |
| Close | Objection handling guide, case studies |
| Post-sale | Handoff doc, success plan template |

## Design Principles

**Scannable over comprehensive** — sales calls are fast. Information must be accessible in seconds, not minutes.

**Situation-specific** — generic collateral gets ignored. Customize by persona, industry, and deal stage.

**Outcome-focused** — every claim connects to revenue, efficiency, or risk reduction. Never just list features.

## Battle Card Structure

```
## [Competitor Name]

**When they come up:** [Signals in discovery that indicate this competitor]

**Their pitch:** [How they position themselves]

**Our counter:** [Our strongest counter, specific and honest]

**Where we win:** [3 specific scenarios where we beat them]

**Where they win:** [1-2 honest scenarios where they're better — know this before reps do]

**Trap-setting questions:** [Discovery questions that expose their weaknesses]

**Proof points:** [Customer story or stat that validates our position]
```

## ROI Calculator Framework

Input variables (customer provides):
- Current [metric] (baseline)
- Team size or volume
- Time spent on [activity]

Output calculations:
- Time saved: [hours/week × $X/hour]
- Revenue impact: [based on their numbers]
- Payback period: [cost ÷ monthly value]

Keep inputs to 3–5 max. More complexity = abandoned.

## Quality Check

- [ ] A rep can use this without asking questions
- [ ] Written in customer language, not internal jargon
- [ ] Claims are specific and verifiable
- [ ] Competitor comparisons are honest
- [ ] Rep-tested before distribution
