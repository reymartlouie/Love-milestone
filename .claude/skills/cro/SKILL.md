# Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

Analyze and improve conversion rates across marketing pages and flows.

## The 5-Second Test

Visitors should understand **what this is and why they should care within 5 seconds**, written in customer language rather than company jargon.

Test: show the page to someone unfamiliar. After 5 seconds, ask:
- What does this product do?
- Who is it for?
- What should I do next?

If they can't answer, fix clarity before anything else.

## Impact Hierarchy

Fix in this order (highest ROI first):

1. **Value clarity** — does the headline communicate the outcome clearly?
2. **Headline effectiveness** — specific, benefit-focused, customer language?
3. **CTA placement and copy** — above the fold, verb-first, low friction?
4. **Visual hierarchy** — does the eye flow to the CTA naturally?
5. **Trust signals** — logos, testimonials, reviews visible early?
6. **Friction removal** — is anything making it harder to convert?

## Page-Specific Priorities

**Homepage:**
- Clear category + primary benefit in headline
- Single CTA above the fold
- Social proof (logos or metrics) within first scroll

**Landing page:**
- No navigation links (remove escape routes)
- Headline matches the ad/source that sent traffic
- Everything on the page supports the single CTA

**Pricing page:**
- Show highest tier first (anchor pricing)
- Highlight recommended plan
- Address top objections inline (FAQ below pricing)
- Annual discount prominently shown

## Quick Wins Checklist

- [ ] Headline uses customer language, not company jargon
- [ ] Primary CTA is visible without scrolling
- [ ] CTA copy is verb-first ("Start free trial" not "Free trial")
- [ ] Social proof appears in first two sections
- [ ] Form has minimum required fields only
- [ ] Mobile layout is clean and CTA is thumb-accessible
- [ ] Page load time under 3 seconds

## Output Format

1. **Quick wins** — changes executable today with high confidence
2. **High-impact changes** — bigger changes worth building and testing
3. **Test ideas** — hypotheses to validate via A/B testing
