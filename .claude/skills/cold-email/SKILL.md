# Cold Email

Write cold emails that get replies. Sharp, human, brief.

## Before Writing

Gather:
1. The specific person and their role
2. Desired outcome (meeting, reply, intro, trial)
3. The value you provide to *their specific* situation
4. Proof points (results, case studies, relevant credibility)
5. Research signals (funding, job postings, recent news, LinkedIn)

Work with whatever context you have — don't let missing info block progress.

## Core Principles

- **Peer-level tone** — write like a smart colleague, not a sales rep
- **Ruthless brevity** — every sentence must earn its place
- **Reader-focused personalization** — observations connect naturally to the ask, not grafted on
- **Single low-friction ask** — interest-based CTAs work better than meeting requests

## Proven Structures

**Observation → Problem → Proof → Ask**
```
[Specific thing you noticed about them] →
[Problem that creates] →
[How you've solved it for similar people] →
[One soft question]
```

**Trigger → Insight → Ask**
```
[Recent event: funding, launch, hiring] →
[Relevant insight about what that means for them] →
[Natural connection to your offer] →
[One question]
```

## Subject Lines

Short, boring, internal-looking. 2–4 words, lowercase.

- `quick question`
- `[company] + [your company]`
- `idea for [their goal]`
- `[mutual connection] suggested I reach out`

Avoid: "Exciting opportunity", "Following up", anything that reads like a mass blast.

## The Ask

**Good (low friction):**
- "Worth a quick conversation?"
- "Is this on your radar?"
- "Would it be useful to see how [X] worked for [similar company]?"

**Bad (high friction):**
- "Can we schedule a 30-minute call this week?"
- "Are you free Tuesday at 2pm?"

## What to Avoid

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- Jargon: "synergy", "leverage", "circle back", "touch base"
- Feature dumps (3+ sentences about your product)
- "Just checking in" follow-ups
- Long intros about your company before the value

## Follow-Up Sequence

- **Day 1:** Original email
- **Day 3:** Add one new piece of value (insight, case study, relevant news)
- **Day 7:** Bump the thread with a short "still relevant?" message
- **Day 14:** Breakup email — "I'll stop reaching out after this"

Max 4 touchpoints. If no reply after 4, stop.
