# Email Sequences

Design automated email flows that deliver value and convert.

**Core rule: One email, one job.** Each message has a single primary purpose and CTA.

## Sequence Types & Structure

| Sequence | Length | Timing | Goal |
|----------|--------|--------|------|
| Welcome | 5–7 emails | Over 2 weeks | Establish relationship + first action |
| Lead nurture | 6–8 emails | Over 2–3 weeks | Move from interest to trial/purchase |
| Onboarding | 4–6 emails | Over 1–2 weeks | Reach activation milestone |
| Re-engagement | 3–4 emails | Over 1 week | Win back inactive subscribers |
| Post-purchase | 3–5 emails | Over 2 weeks | Reduce churn, drive expansion |

## Welcome Sequence (5 emails)

1. **Day 0** — Immediate welcome, deliver the promised value, set expectations
2. **Day 2** — Most important thing to know / do first
3. **Day 5** — Social proof + use case story
4. **Day 9** — Overcome the most common objection
5. **Day 14** — Soft CTA to upgrade/purchase/schedule

## Timing Rules

**B2B:** Avoid weekends. Best days: Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am in recipient's timezone.
**B2C:** Can test weekends. Best times vary — A/B test.

Match frequency to relationship stage: daily is fine for onboarding, weekly or less for nurture.

## Copy Principles

- Short paragraphs (1–3 sentences max)
- Mobile-first — 150–500 words depending on content type
- Conversational tone — write like a person, not a company
- One primary CTA per email, above the fold if possible
- Deliver value before making asks

## Subject Line Formula

| Type | Formula | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Curiosity | "[Specific outcome] without [common pain]" | "More pipeline without cold calls" |
| Direct | "[Thing they want]" | "Your onboarding checklist" |
| Personal | "Re: [previous context]" | "Re: your free trial" |
| Question | "[Question they're asking themselves]" | "Is your trial still working?" |

## Platform Integration

Common tools: Mailchimp, Customer.io, Resend, HubSpot, ConvertKit

Key setup checklist:
- [ ] SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured
- [ ] Unsubscribe link in every email
- [ ] Plain-text version enabled
- [ ] Segment lists by behavior, not just demographics
