# AI SEO

Get your content cited in AI-generated answers, not just ranked in search.

**The distinction:** Traditional SEO gets you ranked. AI SEO gets you **cited**.

## Three-Pillar Strategy

| Pillar | Focus | Tactics |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Structure** | Make content extractable | 40–60 word answer blocks, tables, FAQs |
| **Authority** | Make content citable | Cite sources, add statistics, expert quotes |
| **Presence** | Be where AI looks | Wikipedia, Reddit, reviews, YouTube, third-party |

## Key Stats

- AI Overviews appear in ~45% of Google searches
- Optimized content gets cited **3x more often**
- Statistics + citations boost visibility by **40%+**
- Keyword stuffing **actively hurts** AI visibility (-10%)

## What TO Do

- Write for humans first; organize for clarity
- Add structured data (schema markup)
- Include dated statistics with sources ("According to [Source], 2024...")
- Create comparison tables for competitive queries
- Write 40–60 word direct-answer blocks at the top of sections
- Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt: `GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Googlebot`
- Add `/pricing.md` or pricing page for AI agents evaluating your product
- Use FAQ sections with direct question + answer format
- Get cited on third-party sites (reviews, directories, press)

## What NOT to Do

- Write separate "AI-only" content — optimize existing content instead
- Chunk pages into tiny fragments hoping AI will pick them up
- Block AI crawlers if you want citation
- Gate your best content behind paywalls
- Use keyword stuffing
- Skip freshness signals (update dates, author info)

## Content Format for Maximum Extraction

```markdown
## [Question the user is asking]

[40–60 word direct answer to the question, written as a standalone paragraph.]

[Detailed explanation with statistics, sources, examples...]

| Column A | Column B | Column C |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Data     | Data     | Data     |
```

## Monitoring AI Visibility

Test your key queries in:
- ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini (are you cited?)
- Google with AI Overviews enabled
- Perplexity

Track: citation frequency, source attribution, accuracy of what's cited.
