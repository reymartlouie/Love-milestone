# Free Tools Strategy

Plan and build free tools that generate leads and drive SEO traffic.

## Why Free Tools Work

- High intent: people using a calculator are actively solving a problem
- SEO: tools rank for long-tail keywords and earn backlinks
- Virality: people share tools that save them time
- Lead capture: natural gate after value is delivered

## Ideation Framework

Good free tool ideas come from:
1. Questions your customers ask repeatedly
2. Calculations they currently do in spreadsheets
3. Audits or diagnostics that reveal a problem your product solves
4. Templates they'd customize and reuse

**Validation questions:**
- Does this problem exist at sufficient scale? (keyword volume check)
- Can we solve it in a tool vs. just an article?
- Does this naturally lead to our product?
- Can we build an MVP in < 2 weeks?

## Tool Types

| Type | Example | Effort | Lead Quality |
|------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Calculator | "SaaS pricing calculator" | Low | High (buying intent) |
| Generator | "Job description generator" | Medium | Medium |
| Analyzer / Audit | "SEO audit tool" | High | High |
| Template builder | "Email sequence builder" | Medium | High |
| Comparison tool | "Plan comparison table" | Low | High |

## Gating Strategy

**Option A — Gate upfront**
Email required before using tool. Higher friction, lower starts, higher lead quality.

**Option B — Gate results**
Free to use, email required for full results/export. Better conversion, lower friction.

**Option C — No gate**
Pure SEO/awareness play. Use when lead capture isn't the goal.

**Recommended for most tools:** Option B — deliver partial value free, gate the most valuable output.

## Scoring a Tool Idea

Score 1–5 on each:
- **Search demand:** keyword volume for the problem
- **Lead quality:** how well does this qualify buyers?
- **Build feasibility:** can this be built with available resources?
- **Viral potential:** would users share or bookmark this?
- **Product alignment:** does using this naturally lead to your product?

Build tools scoring 15+ out of 25.

## Launch Checklist

- [ ] Tool solves a real, specific problem
- [ ] Email capture positioned after value delivery
- [ ] SEO: dedicated URL, title tag, meta description
- [ ] Sharing mechanism (share result button)
- [ ] Lead nurture sequence connected to tool topic
- [ ] Analytics tracking on starts, completions, and captures
