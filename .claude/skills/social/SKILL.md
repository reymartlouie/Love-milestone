# Social Content

Create, schedule, and optimize social content across platforms.

## Before Starting

Check `.agents/product-marketing.md`. Then clarify:
- Primary goal (awareness, leads, traffic, community)
- Target audience and which platforms they're on
- Brand voice and tone
- Available time per week
- Content already being produced (blog, podcast, video)

## Content Pillars

Define 3–5 pillars aligned with audience interests:

| Pillar Type | Purpose | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Educational | Teach something valuable | "How to [do X]" |
| Behind-the-scenes | Build trust and humanize | "How we built [feature]" |
| Social proof | Demonstrate results | Customer wins, metrics |
| Opinion/Contrarian | Drive engagement | "Hot take: [unpopular view]" |
| Product | Direct promotion | Feature announcements, demos |

Ratio: ~60% educational/value + 20% behind-scenes + 20% product/proof.

## Platform Guidance

| Platform | Format | Frequency | Best For |
|----------|--------|-----------|---------|
| LinkedIn | Long-form text + carousels | 3–5x/week | B2B, thought leadership |
| Twitter/X | Short text, threads | 1–3x/day | Developer tools, building in public |
| Instagram | Visual + Reels | 4–7x/week | Consumer, visual products |
| TikTok | Short video (15–60s) | Daily | Consumer, younger audience |
| Facebook | Mixed, video | 3–5x/week | Consumer, community |

## Hook Formulas

Every post needs a scroll-stopping first line:

- **Curiosity:** "Most [audience] don't know [surprising thing]"
- **Story:** "Last [year/month/week], I [surprising event]"
- **Value:** "[Number] ways to [desirable outcome]"
- **Contrarian:** "Unpopular opinion: [challenge common belief]"
- **Question:** "What would you do if [scenario]?"

## Content Repurposing System

One long-form piece → multiple posts:

```
Blog post / podcast / webinar
  → LinkedIn long-form post (key insight)
  → Twitter thread (10 points)
  → Instagram carousel (visual takeaways)
  → TikTok/Reel (talking head summary)
  → Email newsletter section
```

Target 3–5 posts per blog post or podcast episode.

## Short-Form Video (3-Second Rule)

First 3 seconds must have:
- Visual hook (something unexpected or dynamic)
- Verbal hook (spoken opening line)
- Text overlay (captions always on)

## Weekly Time Budget

2–3 hours/week is enough if batching:
- 1 hour: write and schedule all text posts
- 1 hour: record short-form videos in batch
- 30 min: engage (reply to comments, respond to DMs)

## Metrics by Goal

| Goal | Primary Metric |
|------|---------------|
| Awareness | Impressions, reach |
| Engagement | Likes, comments, shares |
| Traffic | Link clicks |
| Leads | Profile visits → signups |
