# Customer Research

Uncover authentic customer insights from existing data or online communities.

## Two Modes

**Mode 1 — Analyze existing research**
Transcripts, surveys, support tickets, NPS responses, reviews

**Mode 2 — Gather intel from online communities**
Reddit, G2, Capterra, forums, Twitter/X — where customers speak without filters

## Mode 1: Analyzing Existing Data

### What to Extract

For each source, pull:
- **Jobs to be done** — what are they trying to accomplish?
- **Pain points** — what frustrates or blocks them?
- **Trigger events** — what caused them to look for a solution?
- **Desired outcomes** — what does success look like to them?
- **Exact language** — verbatim phrases they use (gold for copywriting)

### Confidence Levels

Label every insight:
- **High** — mentioned by 5+ independent sources, consistent framing
- **Medium** — mentioned by 2–4 sources, some variation
- **Low** — single source, may be outlier

### Sample Bias Awareness

| Source | Bias |
|--------|------|
| Online reviews | Skew toward power users and extreme experiences |
| Support tickets | Skew toward problems (not wins) |
| NPS detractors | Skew toward recent bad experience |
| Sales call transcripts | Skew toward people who were already interested |

## Mode 2: Online Community Intel

### Where to Look

- **Reddit:** search `site:reddit.com "[problem/category]"`
- **G2/Capterra:** filter reviews by rating, read 1-2 star for pain, 5-star for wins
- **Twitter/X:** search problem keywords + your category
- **Hacker News:** search `[category] site:news.ycombinator.com`
- **Indie Hackers / Product Hunt:** comments on competitor products

### Search Queries

```
"[problem]" site:reddit.com
"[competitor name] alternative"
"[job title] struggling with [area]"
"[category] frustrating"
"switched from [competitor] to"
```

## Synthesis Template

```markdown
## Key Insight: [Theme]
**Confidence:** High / Medium / Low
**Evidence count:** [N] independent sources

**What they say (verbatim):**
> "[exact quote]" — [source]
> "[exact quote]" — [source]

**What it means:**
[Your interpretation]

**Marketing implication:**
[How to use this in copy/positioning]
```

## Building Personas

Only after validating 5–10+ data points per segment:
- Demographics + firmographics
- Primary job to be done
- Top 3 pain points (with confidence)
- Trigger events
- Objections
- Language patterns

Never build personas from assumptions — only from validated research patterns.
