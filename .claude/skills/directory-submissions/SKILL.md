# Directory Submissions

Build compounding backlinks and discovery through strategic directory submissions.

## Phase 0: Readiness Check

Before submitting anywhere:
- [ ] Landing page live with clear value prop
- [ ] Screenshots and demo video ready
- [ ] Positioning document ready (see `/product-marketing`)
- [ ] 5–10 beta users who can provide reviews
- [ ] Support email set up

## Submission Tiers

### Tier 1 — Flagship (highest impact)
- **Product Hunt** — biggest launch moment for tech products
- **G2** — most important for B2B; drives reviews + SEO
- **Capterra** — B2B software directory

### Tier 2 — High-Value
- Indie Hackers, Hacker News (Show HN)
- BetaList (pre-launch discovery)
- AppSumo (deal marketplace)
- Chrome Web Store (if browser extension)

### Tier 3 — Category-Specific
Submit to directories specific to your category:
- Fintech: Fintech Database, Finovate
- HR: HR Tech, HRExaminer
- Dev tools: Awesome lists, GitHub topics
- AI tools: There's an AI For That, FutureTools
- No-code: No Code List, Makerpad

### Tier 4 — SEO Backlink Directories
- Crunchbase (free tier)
- AngelList
- BuiltWith (claim your profile)
- SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, SoftwareAdvice

## Product Hunt Strategy

**3-week prep timeline:**

Week 1:
- Identify a hunter with 1000+ followers in your category
- Build your supporter list (past users, investors, community)
- Prepare listing: tagline, description, gallery, first comment

Week 2:
- Brief your supporter list on launch date
- Schedule social announcement posts
- Prepare thank-you responses

Week 3 (launch week):
- Launch Tuesday–Thursday (Monday and Friday underperform)
- Launch at 12:01 AM Pacific (start of PH day)
- Engage every comment, all day
- Convert PH traffic → email signup (not just upvotes)

## G2/Capterra Reviews: The 10-in-30 Protocol

Get 10 reviews in 30 days to hit Grid appearance:

1. Email your 20 most engaged users
2. Personal ask: "Would you spend 5 minutes on an honest G2 review?"
3. Include direct link to review page
4. Follow up once if no response after 7 days
5. Thank everyone who reviews (private, not on G2)

**Never:** offer incentives for positive reviews (violates platform terms, destroys credibility)

## AI Citation Sources (GEO)

Get cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity:
- Wikipedia: get your product mentioned in category articles
- Reddit: participate in relevant subreddits organically
- YouTube: create category-defining content
- Third-party blogs and review sites
- GitHub: contribute to relevant open-source projects
